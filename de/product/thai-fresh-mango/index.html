Nam Dok Mai is the mango Thais eat with sticky rice: long, slender and honey-sweet with smooth, fibre-free flesh and a floral scent.

For Japan, Korea, Australia, New Zealand and other markets with strict quarantine rules, our mangoes go through vapour heat treatment in the packhouse — 47 °C for 20 minutes at more than 90 % relative humidity — before grading, packing and shipment.
