La empresa

Un socio de confianza en la exportación de frescos tailandeses

Tropical Green Co., Ltd. es un exportador con sede en Bangkok de frutas, verduras y alimentos procesados de Tailandia, fundado en 2014 y registrado ante el Departamento de Promoción del Comercio Internacional.

Nuestra historia

De una oficina en Rama II Road a importadores de cuatro continentes

Tropical Green began in 2014 with a simple brief: bring the fruit Thailand is famous for — Monthong durian, Nam Dok Mai mango, mangosteen, Nam Hom coconut — to buyers abroad in the condition people taste it here.

We do not try to own every orchard. We work closely with a select group of partner farms, agree on varieties and harvest maturity in advance, and take responsibility from the moment the fruit is cut. Our packhouse in Samut Sakhon handles treatment, grading and packing; our Bangkok office handles documents, logistics and the conversation with every importer.

Over the years the catalogue has grown from fresh fruit to fresh-cut, vegetables and herbs, an organic programme with our partner King Fresh Farm, and our own mango and mangosteen juices.
