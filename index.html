The company

A certified Thai exporter delivering consistent quality from farm to destination.

Since 2014 we have worked with a close circle of partner farms, picked by hand at export maturity and controlled temperature from packhouse to port. We are a registered exporter with Thailand’s Department of International Trade Promotion, certified to GMP, GAP and HACCP.
