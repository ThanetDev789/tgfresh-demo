Thailand grows dozens of mango varieties, but two dominate export: Nam Dok Mai, eaten ripe, and green mango, eaten crisp and sour.

Seasons

The main Nam Dok Mai harvest runs from March to June, with off-season fruit available in smaller volumes through the year. Green mango peaks in the cooler months.

Vapour heat treatment

Japan, Korea, Australia, New Zealand and other markets require mangoes to be free of fruit fly. Vapour heat treatment does this without chemicals: fruit is held at a core temperature of 47 °C for 20 minutes at more than 90 % relative humidity, then cooled, graded and packed.

Getting it right

Treatment must happen in an approved facility, and paperwork must match the lot. Work with an exporter who runs treatment in-house and can show the records.
